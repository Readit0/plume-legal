## Inqaku 6 — Ii-Quota

Ukusetyenziswa kwenkonzo kunqunyelwe, ukuze kulawulwe iindleko zokucuta.

**Inqanaba elisimahla.** Unomda osisiseko wokulungiswa kombhalo ngosuku, ngaphandle kokubukela nantoni na. Xa lo mda usugqityiwe, unokw**khetha** ukubukela isibhengezo esinomvuzo ukuze uvule ukusetyenziswa okongezelelweyo, phantsi komda wenani eliphezulu lezibhengezo ngosuku nomda opheleleyo wosuku. Ukubukela isibhengezo **akufanelekanga nanini na**: ukuba awukwenzi oko, ugcina nje oko ulungelelanisiweyo kuko, uze ufumane umda wakho ngomso.

*Ngomhla wokubhalwa kwesi sixhobo, amaxabiso asetyenzisiweyo ngala: umda wentlungiswano ezi-5 ngosuku, +5 ngesibhengezo ngasinye esinomvuzo esiqinisekiswiweyo, izibhengezo ezi-5 ngosuku ubukhulu becala, umda opheleleyo we-30 ngosuku.*

**Inqanaba lomrhumi.** Umrhumo ususa umda wenqanaba elisimahla kwaye ususa izibhengezo.

- Xa ukucuta kwenzeka **kwidivayisi yakho** (iidivayisi ezihambelana ne-AI yasekhaya): **ukusetyenziswa okungenammda**, phantsi kwemida yobuchwepheshe yefowuni yakho kuphela (ibhetri, ukushisa).
- Xa ukucuta kwenzeka **kwiiseva zethu**: ukusetyenziswa kunqunyelwe ngosuku nangenyanga, ngenxa yokuba isicelo ngasinye sinendleko yokwenene. *Ngomhla wokubhalwa: 1 000 ngosuku kunye ne-9 000 ngenyanga.*

**Izinto eziqhelekileyo kuwo onke amanqanaba:**

- **Ukuhlalutya iskrini** (uguqulelo okanye ingcebiso yempendulo) kubiza ngaphezulu kunentlungiswano elula kwaye kusebenzisa **iiyunithi ezininzi ze-quota** — okwangoku 5.
- Ii-quota **ziqhutywa yiiseva zethu**, phambi kokucuta konke, kwaye zibuyela kuqanda xa usuku (ixesha le-UTC) okanye inyanga itshintsha.
- Ii-quota zidibene **ne-akhawunti yakho**, hayi idivayisi yakho: ukutshintsha ifowuni okanye ukufaka kwakhona i-app akuzibuyiseli kuqanda.

**La maxabiso anokutshintsha.** Sinokuwalungisa ukuze sigcine ubunzulu bezoqoqosho benkonzo okanye xa iindleko zabanikezeli be-AI zitshintsha. **Amanani asebenzayo ngawo aboniswe kwiapp**, angawona anegunya: umlinganiselo we-quota kunye neskrini somrhumo zibonisa ibhalansi yakho yokwenyani nanini na. Nasiphi na isihlisiyo esibalulekileyo kwii-quota zomrhumo osaqhubekayo siya kukwaziswa kuwe kwangaphambili size sikunike ilungelo lokurhoxa ngaphandle kweendleko.
