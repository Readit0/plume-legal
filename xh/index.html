Izimbo zakho, hayi ezohlu

Apha, isimbo sokubhala sibizwa ngokuba yi-persona. Ezinye zinikwa simahla, ezinye zikulindele kwikhathalogi.

Ezinikwa simahla

Ngokuthe ngqo

Iya ngqo kwinto. Izivakalisi ezimfutshane, ithoni eqinileyo, akukho kujikeleza. Ilungele izigqibo ezikhawulezayo, iimpendulo zomsebenzi ezingadlaliyo, okanye ukucacisa isicelo.

Ngobuchule

Izolile kwaye icocekile, isigama esilungileyo, imbeko efanelekileyo. Ilungele i-imeyile yomsebenzi, umyalezo weklayenti, okanye nayiphi na imeko efuna imbonakalo entle.

Ngobuhlobo

Ifudumele kwaye isondele, njengomhlobo okumeleyo. Ihlala isemvelweni, yongeze ububele. Ilungele abantu obathandayo, imiyalezo yemihla ngemihla, ukudambisa ngoncumo.

Ngokufutshane

Icuthiwe yaya kwesona sisiseko, ingayilahli intsingiselo. Isusa okungenamsebenzi, igcine ingongoma. Ilungele iimpendulo ezikhawulezayo, izaziso, xa igama ngalinye libalulekile.

Ngothando

Inobubele kwaye inyanisekile, imvakalelo ithethwa ngamagama alula. Iyafudumeza ingagqithisi, igcine ukuzeyisa. Ilungele umyalezo wothando, inqaku elimnandi, isibhambathiso esivakala sinyanisekile.

Ubungcali bomsebenzi Ngokubhalisa

Idiplomathi

Ibhala kwakhona ngobuchule nangobudiplomatsi. Ithambisa imiyalezo enoxinzelelo, igcine intsingiselo kodwa ilungise indlela ethethwa ngayo. Ilungele iincoko ezinobunzima, iiklayenti ezingonwabanga, ukungavisisani.

Inkonzo yabathengi

Inobuchule, inovelwano kwaye ijolise kwisisombululo. Iyayamkela ingxaki, iyakhuthaza, inikeze inyathelo elilandelayo elicacileyo. Ilungele inkxaso, izikhalazo, kunye nenkonzo emva kokuthenga.

Ingcibi · uxabiso

Icacile, ibambekayo kwaye iyakhuthaza ngomsebenzi. Ichaza umsebenzi, ibeke ixabiso nexesha ngokuzithemba. Ilungele ukuphendula iklayenti, ukuthumela uxabiso, ukucacisa inkonzo.

Umthethathethwano ozolileyo

Iqinile kwintsingiselo, ithambile kwindlela. Ikhusela isimo sakho ngaphandle koxinzelelo, ifune indawo yokuvumelana. Ilungele ukuxoxa ngexabiso, ukubeka umda, ukusombulula umbango.

Isaziso sentengiso

Yenza abantu bafune ngemigca embalwa. Ibalaselisa imveliso, ineenyaniso kwaye itsalayo, kunye nomyalelo ocacileyo wokwenza. Ilungele ukuthengisa kwi-intanethi, isaziso sentengiso, into esele isetyenzisiwe.

Ubuyili nesimbo Ngokubhalisa

Uncwadi olucikizekileyo

Iphakamisa isimbo: isigama esityebileyo, izivakalisi ezihamba kakuhle, ubunono obulawulwayo. Ilungele umbhalo ocikizekileyo, ileta ekhunjulwayo, ingxelo enobunono.

Ubuqili nempendulo ebukhali

Ibukhali kwaye inobuqili, inentwana yokuhlekisa ebekwe kakuhle. Iphendula ngobuchule, ingaze yenzakalise. Ilungele ukuvuselela incoko, ukuphendula ngobuhle, ukukhazimla ekubhaleni.

Ubuqhawe nembeko

Inesidima kwaye inembeko, ithoni yozuko nentlonipho. Ibeka izinto ngobukhulu nangobuqhawe. Ilungele umyalezo onzulu, umbulelo ophuma entliziyweni, ithoni yeqonga oyithathayo ngabom.

Ubumbongi

Imifanekiso nomculo: izifaniso ezithambileyo, isingqi esenziweyo. Iguqula okuqhelekileyo kube kuhle. Ilungele inqaku elinobubele, umnikelo, umyalezo ofikelelayo.

Ubumnyama obunesidima

Imnyama kwaye icikizekile, imfihlakalo namandla alawulwayo. Inomdlalo kodwa izeyisa. Ilungele imeko-mphefumlo eqinileyo, umbhalo onesimilo, utyikityo olubethayo.

Ubuncinane

Ukucoceka okugqibeleleyo: amagama ambalwa, intsingiselo eninzi. Igama ngalinye likhethiwe. Ilungele imbali emfutshane, isaci, umyalezo wanamhlanje ococekileyo.

Ukutolika

Ngaphezu kokubhala kwakhona: ithatha injongo yakho iyiveze icace. Ilungele xa umbhalo wakho ungekacokiswa kwaye ufuna ukutsho izinto kanye ngendlela oyifunayo.
