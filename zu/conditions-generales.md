## Isigaba 6 — Isabelo

Ukusetshenziswa kwesevisi kunomkhawulo, ukuze kulawulwe izindleko zokucubungula.

**Izinga lamahhala.** Unenani eliyisisekelo lokubuyekeza ngosuku, ngaphandle kokubuka lutho. Uma leli nani seliphelile, **ungakhetha** ukubuka isikhangiso esinomvuzo ukuvula ukusetshenziswa okwengeziwe, ngaphakathi komkhawulo wenani eliphakeme lezikhangiso ngosuku kanye nomkhawulo opheleleyo wosuku. Ukubuka isikhangiso **akudingekile nanini**: uma ungakwenzi, ugcina nje lokho onalo ilungelo lakho, futhi uthola inani lakho lesisekelo kusasa.

*Ngosuku lokubhalwa kwalokhu, amanani asetshenziswayo yila: isisekelo sokubuyekeza okungu-5 ngosuku, +5 ngesikhangiso ngasinye esinomvuzo esiqinisekisiwe, izikhangiso ezingu-5 kakhulu ngosuku, umkhawulo opheleleyo wosuku ongu-30.*

**Izinga lababhalisele.** Ukubhalisela kususa umkhawulo wezinga lamahhala futhi kususa izikhangiso.

- Uma ukucubungula kwenzeka **edivayisini yakho** (amadivayisi ahambisana ne-Local AI): **ukusetshenziswa okungenamkhawulo**, ngaphandle kwemikhawulo yobuchwepheshe yefoni yakho (ibhethri, ukushisa).
- Uma ukucubungula kwenzeka **kumaseva ethu**: ukusetshenziswa kunomkhawulo ngosuku nangenyanga, ngoba ucingo ngalunye lunentengo yangempela. *Ngosuku lokubhalwa: 1,000 ngosuku no-9,000 ngenyanga.*

**Amaphuzu ajwayelekile kuwo wonke amazinga:**

- **Ukuhlaziya isikrini** (ukuhumusha noma isiphakamiso sempendulo) kubiza kakhulu kunokubuyekeza okulula futhi kudla **amayunithi amaningi esabelo** — okwamanje angu-5.
- Isabelo **sisetshenziswa ngamaseva ethu**, ngaphambi kwanoma yikuphi ukucubungula, futhi sisetshelwa kabusha kuzero uma usuku (isikhathi se-UTC) noma inyanga kushintsha.
- Isabelo sixhunywe **ku-akhawunti yakho**, hhayi edivayisini yakho: ukushintsha ifoni noma ukufaka kabusha uhlelo lokusebenza akusethuli kabusha kuzero.

**Lawa manani angase ashintshe.** Singawalungisa ukuze kugcinwe ukulingana kwezomnotho kwesevisi noma uma kwenzeka ushintsho entengweni yabahlinzeki be-AI. **Izinombolo ezisebenzayo yilezo eziboniswe ohlelweni lokusebenza**, okuyizona okuncikwa kuzo: isilinganiso sesabelo kanye nesikrini sokubhalisela sikubonisa ibhalansi yakho yangempela nganoma yisiphi isikhathi. Noma yikuphi ukuncishiswa okukhulu kwesabelo sokubhalisela okuqhubekayo kuzokwaziswa kuwe ngaphambili futhi kukunike ilungelo lokukhansela ngaphandle kwezindleko.

**Ukuvuselelwa ngokuzenzakalelayo.** **Ukubhalisela kwakho** kuzovuselelwa **ngokuzenzakalelayo** ekupheleni kwesikhathi ngasinye (ngenyanga noma ngonyaka), ngenani elisebenzayo ngaleso sikhathi, ngaphandle uma ukhansela **okungenani amahora angu-24 ngaphambi** kokuphela kwesikhathi samanje. Ukuvuselelwa kukhokhwa yi-Google endleleni yakho yokukhokha.

**Ukukhansela.** Ukhansela nanoma nini, **kusukela ku-Google Play** (Play Store → Imenyu → Izinkokhelo Nokubhalisela → Ukubhalisela → Plume → Khansela). I-Google iyilawula ukubhalisela: asikwazi ukukhansela esikhundleni sakho. Ukukhansela kusebenza **ekupheleni kwesikhathi esesikhokhelwe**: ugcina ukufinyelela kuze kube yileso sikhathi, futhi ngemva kwalokho awukhokhiswa.

**Ushintsho lwenani.** Noma yikuphi ukwenyuka kuzokwaziswa nguGoogle ngaphambi kokusebenza futhi kudinga ukuvuma kwakho, uma kungenjalo ukubhalisela kuzophela.

**Ukuphela kokubhalisela.** Ekupheleni, i-akhawunti yakho ibuyela ezingeni lamahhala. Awulahlekelwa i-akhawunti yakho, amapersona akho, noma izilungiselelo zakho.
