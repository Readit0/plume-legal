I-Plume ikusiza ukuthi ubhale: ibuyekeza umbhalo wakho ngqo ohlelweni lokusebenza obhala kulo, futhi ingahumusha umbhalo obonakala esikrinini.

1. **I-Plume ayigcini imibhalo oyibuyekezayo, noma umbhalo ofundwe esikrinini.** Asigcini ikhophi noma irekhodi. **Isaphulelo esaziwayo, esenziwe ngamabomu:** uma udala **ulimi lomuntu siqu** (ulimi lwakho oluzenzele lona, olunesichazamazwi samagama nezincazelo zawo), okuqukethwe kwalolo limi **kuyagcinwa** kumaseva ethu — yiyona ndlela eyodwa yokukuvumela ukuthi uluthole kwenye idivayisi, uluthuthukise, futhi ulwabelane. Imininingwane iku-§3.
2. **Kuya ngokuthi injini yini oyikhethayo, umbhalo wakho uyahamba noma awuhambi efonini yakho.** Izinjini ezimbili (I-Kit Yasekhaya nobuChwepheshe Bokusebenza Ngokwengqondo Basekhaya) zisebenza ngokugcwele edivayisini. Eyesithathu (I-Cloud AI) ithumela umbhalo kunsiza yobuchwepheshe bokusebenza ngokwengqondo **etholakala ngaphandle kweNyunyana yaseYurophu**. Wena ukhetha, futhi i-Cloud AI ayisebenzi ngaphandle kwemvume yakho ecacile.
3. **I-Plume idinga izimvume ezinamandla** (ukufunda okuqukethwe okubonakala kwezinye izinhlelo zokusebenza, ukuthatha isithombe sesikrini). Ngezansi sichaza ngokuqondile ukuthi zisetshenziselwani nokuthi zingasetshenziselwa yini.

## 1. Lokho i-Plume ikufundayo esikrinini sakho, nokuthi nini

### 1.1 Isevisi yokufinyeleleka

Ukuze ibuyekeze umbhalo wakho lapho uwubhala khona, i-Plume isebenzisa isevisi yokufinyeleleka ye-Android. Le yimvume oyivula wena ngokwakho, ezilungiselelweni zefoni, ngemva kwesikrini sencazelo i-Plume ekukhombisa **ngaphambi** kokuyicela.

Ngokuqondile:

- **Uma iphumula**, i-Plume yazi kuphela ukuthi yiluphi uhlelo lokusebenza oluvuliwe nokuthi nini ubeka ikhathini endaweni yokufaka. Yilokhu okubangela ikapula elintantayo ukuthi livele — futhi kuphela kuzinhlelo zokusebenza wena ngokwakho ozimisele.
- **Okuqukethwe kwendawo kufundwa kuphela ngesikhathi uthinta ikapula**, ukuze kubuyekezwe bese kubuyiswa kuleyo ndawo.
- **Izindawo zamagama-mfihlo azifakiwe.** Uhlelo lokusebenza luyazithola izindawo zohlobo lwegama-mfihlo (kuhlanganise namakhodi wezinombolo nezindawo zewebhu), luenqaba ukuzifunda.
- Le mvume **ayivumeli noma yikuphi ukuthathwa kwesithombe** sesikrini sakho.
- I-Plume **ayicindezeli esikhundleni sakho** kolunye uhlelo lokusebenza: ibuyisela umbhalo wendawo, akukho lutho olunye.

Imisebenzi emibili oyivula wena ngokwakho — **Ukufunda Okusizayo ngesimo Sombhalo** kanye **nokuhumusha izigxathu ezitholakele** — ifunda umbhalo obonakalayo ngokuqhubekayo uma isebenza, futhi imiswa ngokushesha uma uyivala.

Uma wenqaba isevisi yokufinyeleleka, i-Plume isasebenza: ungakhetha umbhalo bese usebenzisa imenyu yokukhetha "Plume" ye-Android, noma wabelane ngombhalo ne-Plume.

### 1.2 Ukuthatha isithombe sesikrini (Ukufunda Okusizayo)

Ukufunda Okusizayo kubeka inguqulo phezu kombhalo obonakalayo — isibonelo, amabhamuza wamahlaya. Kudinga ukubona isithombe sesikrini.

- **Kucinywe ngokuzenzakalelayo** futhi kusebenza kuphela ezinhlelweni zokusebenza ozivumele ngokusobala, ngayinye ngayinye.
- **I-Android icela imvume yayo yona ngokwayo kuqala ngakunye kwesigcawu.** Lokhu akusiyona imvume enikezwa kanye kuze kube phakade: isigcawu ngasinye sidinga imvume entsha. I-Plume ayizami ukusebenzisa kabusha noma ukugwema le mvume.
- Kuso sonke isigcawu, **isaziso esihlala njalo nesibonisi sesistimu kuhlala kubonakala**. I-Plume ayikwazi ukuthatha isithombe sesikrini sakho ngasese.
- Isigcawu **sima ngokuzenzakalelayo lapho isikrini sikhiywa**, futhi ngokushesha uma uwumisa wena ngokwakho.
- Izinhlelo zokusebenza ezivikela isibonakaliso sazo (izinhlelo zebhange, abaphathi bamagama-mfihlo) **zicashiswa yi-Android ngokwayo** ngaphambi kokuthi i-Plume ithole noma yini. Lokhu ukuvikelwa kwesistimu okuyiqiniso kodwa okungaphelele: azizona zonke izinhlelo zokusebenza ezibucayi ezivula lo msebenzi. Ngakho ke asikwethuli njengesiqinisekiso esiphelele.
- **Izithombe ezithathiwe azigcinwa noma zithunyelwe.** Isithombe ngasinye sihlaziywa enkumbulweni ukuze kukhishwe umbhalo, bese kulahlwa. Asikho isithombe esiphuma efonini yakho, nanini, kungakhathaliseki ukuthi iyiphi injini ekhethiwe.

## 2. Lokho okusala efonini yakho nalokho okuhambayo

Lona ngumehluko obaluleke kakhulu kule nqubomgomo, futhi nguwe olawulayo.

### 2.1 Izinjini ezingathumeli lutho ngaphandle

- **I-Kit Yasekhaya** (ukubona nokuhumusha umbhalo ngaphandle kwe-inthanethi) isebenza ngokugcwele edivayisini.
- **UbuChwepheshe Bokusebenza Ngokwengqondo Basekhaya** iyimodeli yobuchwepheshe bokusebenza ngokwengqondo ekhishwa kanye bese igcinwa efonini yakho (cishe ama-MB angu-720). Isebenza edivayisini yakho.

Ngalezi zinjini ezimbili, **umbhalo ofundiwe noma obuyekeziwe awuhambi efonini yakho.** Alukho ucingo lwenethiwekhi oluphathelene nokuqukethwe kombhalo wakho.

### 2.2 Injini ye-Cloud AI

Uma ukhetha i-Cloud AI, noma uma idivayisi yakho ingenamandla anele ye-Local AI, umbhalo ophathelene uyathunyelwa kumaseva ethu, bese usa kunsiza yobuchwepheshe bokusebenza ngokwengqondo yangaphandle.

**Kubalulekile ukucacisa ngendlela yangempela:**

- Umbhalo udlula ngengqalasizinda yethu yeseva, etholakala **eNyunyaneni yaseYurophu** (isifunda saseYurophu Emaphakathi, iFrankfurt).
- Bese uthunyelwa kumlamuleli wokuqondisa **otholakala ngaphandle kweNyunyana yaseYurophu**, ovumela imodeli ye-AI yenkampani yesithathu ukuthi iwucubungule.
- **Kungakho, lokhu kungukudluliswa kwedatha ngaphandle kweNyunyana yaseYurophu.** Asikuphiki lokhu, futhi asethuli sithembiso sokuqoshwa saseYurophu kulesi sinyathelo.
- **I-Plume ayiwugcini umbhalo wakho.** Awukho umsebenzi wethu weseva obhala okuqukethwe kombhalo wakho: sirekhoda kuphela isibonisi sobuchwepheshe sesicelo nesibonisi sedivayisi yakho, ukuze sibale isabelo sakho futhi sithole ukusetshenziswa okubi.
- **Lokho abahlinzeki laba abakwenzayo ohlangothini lwabo, asikwazi ukukuqinisekisa.** Sithanda ukukutshela lokhu kunokukuthembisa ukugcinwa okulinganiselwa ku-0 esingakwazi ukukuqinisekisa.

**I-Cloud AI ayisebenzi yona ngokwayo nanini.** Isikrini esizinikele sokuvuma sikuchazela lezi zinto ngaphambi kokuthunyelwa kokuqala, futhi akulutho oluhambayo ngaphandle kokuthi uvume. Uma i-Local AI ihluleka, i-Plume ayishintsheli ku-cloud ngasese: iyakwazisa futhi ilinde isinqumo sakho. Ungakwazi ukuhoxisa le mvume nanoma nini ezilungiselelweni.
