Izitayela zakho, hhayi ezohlu

Lapha, isitayela sokubhala sibizwa ngokuthi i-persona. Ezinye zinikezwa mahhala, ezinye zikulindele ekhathalogi.

Ezinikezwa mahhala

Oqondile

Iya ngqo endabeni. Imisho emifushane, ithoni eqinile, akukho ukubhuduza. Ilungele izinqumo ezisheshayo, izimpendulo zomsebenzi ezingadlali, noma ukucacisa isicelo.

Ochwepheshile

Ezolile futhi ecwebile, amagama ahlanzekile, inhlonipho efanele. Ilungele i-imeyili yomsebenzi, umlayezo weklayenti, noma yisiphi isimo lapho isithombe sakho sibalulekile.

Onobungane

Efudumele futhi eseduze, njengomngane okusekelayo. Igcina okwemvelo, yengeze umusa. Ilungele abathandekayo, imilayezo yansuku zonke, ukudambisa ngokumamatheka.

Omfushane

Inciphisiwe kwaba okubalulekile ngaphandle kokulahlekelwa umqondo. Isusa okungadingekile, igcine iphuzu. Ilungele izimpendulo ezisheshayo, izaziso, lapho igama ngalinye libalulekile.

Wothando

Emnene futhi eqotho, imizwa ishiwo ngamagama alula. Iyafudumeza ngaphandle kokweqisa, igcine ukuzithiba. Ilungele umlayezo wothando, incwadi emnandi, isimemezelo esizwakala siqotho.

Ubungcweti bomsebenzi Ngokubhalisa

Onobuchule

Ibeka kabusha ngobuchule nangokucophelela. Ithambisa imilayezo ecindezelayo, igcine umongo kodwa ilungise indlela oshiwo ngayo. Ilungele izingxoxo ezibucayi, amakhasimende anganelisekile, ukungezwani.

Insizakalo yamakhasimende

Enobungcweti, enozwelo futhi egxile esixazululweni. Iyayivuma inkinga, iyaduduza, inikeze isinyathelo esilandelayo esicacile. Ilungele usizo, izikhalazo, nokulandela emva kokuthenga.

Ingcweti · isilinganiso sentengo

Ecacile, ephathekayo futhi eduduzayo ngomsebenzi. Ichaza umsebenzi, ibeke intengo nesikhathi ngokuzethemba. Ilungele ukuphendula iklayenti, ukuthumela isilinganiso sentengo, ukuchaza insizakalo.

Oxoxisana ngokuzola

Eqinile emongweni, ethambile endleleni. Ivikela isikhundla sakho ngaphandle kokucindezela, ifuna indawo evumelanayo. Ilungele ukuxoxa ngentengo, ukubeka umkhawulo, ukusingatha ingxabano.

Isikhangiso sokuthengisa

Yenza abantu bafise ngemigqa embalwa. Igqamisa umkhiqizo, iqinisile futhi ikhangayo, inesicelo esicacile sokwenza. Ilungele ukuthengisa ku-inthanethi, isikhangiso esincane, into esisetshenzisiwe.

Ubuciko nesitayela Ngokubhalisa

Wobuciko obuphakeme

Iphakamisa isitayela: amagama acebile, imisho egelezayo, ubuhle obulawulwayo. Ilungele umbhalo ocwebile, incwadi ekhumbulekayo, isitatimende esicwebile.

Ubuhlakani nempendulo esheshayo

Ebukhali futhi enobuhlakani, nokuhlekisa okubekwe kahle. Iphendula ngobuchule, ingalimazi. Ilungele ukuvuselela ingxoxo, ukuphendula ngesitayela, ukukhazimula ngokubhala.

Wobuqhawe

Ehloniphekile futhi enesizotha, ithoni yodumo nenhlonipho. Ibeka izinto ngobukhulu nangesizotha. Ilungele umlayezo omkhulu, ukubonga okusuka enhliziyweni, ithoni yetiyetha ozikhethele yona.

Wenkondlo

Imifanekiso nomculo: izifaniso ezithambile, isigqi esakhiwe kahle. Iguqula okuvamile kube okuhle. Ilungele incwadi emnene, ukwethulwa, umlayezo ofinyelela enhliziyweni.

Omnyama futhi ocwebile

Emnyama futhi ecwebile, imfihlakalo namandla okulawulwayo. Enodrama kodwa elawulekayo. Ilungele umoya oqinile, umbhalo onobuntu, isiginesha eshaqisayo.

Olula kakhulu

Ukuhlanzeka okuphelele: amagama ambalwa, umqondo omningi. Igama ngalinye likhethiwe. Ilungele ibhayogirafi emfushane, umgqa wokukhangisa, umlayezo ohlanzekile wanamuhla.

Ukuchaza umqondo

Ngaphezu kokubeka kabusha: ithatha injongo yakho bese iyiveza ngokucacile. Ilungele lapho umbhalo wakho ungakalungi bese ufuna ukusho izinto ngendlela efanele.

Ukufunda okusizwayo

Humusha lokho okufundayo: i-manga, i-webtoon, uhlelo lwangaphandle. Ukuhumusha kuvela phezu kombhalo, endaweni yawo: ufunda ngolimi lwakho ungakopi lutho.

I-Plume ikhuluma ulimi lwakho, futhi ihumusha ezinye eziningi.

Lapho umbhalo wakho ucutshungulwa khona, nguwe okhethayo

Izimvume, nokuthi i-Plume ayikwenzi

I-Plume isebenzisa isevisi yokufinyeleleka ye-Android ukufunda nokushintsha umbhalo endaweni obhala kuyo, uma uthinta ikhapsuli, futhi kuphela ezinhlelweni ozixhumile. Ukufunda okusizwayo kucela imvume yakho yokuthatha isikrini njalo uma uqala. Ayithumeli lutho ngasemuva, futhi ayithumeli umyalezo egameni lakho.

Mahhala, ukubhalisa akuphoqelekile

I-Plume isetshenziswa mahhala, ngenani lokubhala kabusha ngosuku; isikhangiso esinomvuzo sivula okwengeziwe, asiphoqwa. Ukubhalisa kukhuphula imikhawulo, kuvula yonke ikhathalogi futhi kususa izikhangiso.
