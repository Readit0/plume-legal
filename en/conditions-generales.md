## Article 2 — What the service does

Plume is a writing-assistance tool that runs on Android. It allows you:

- to **rewrite a text** that you are writing, directly in the application where you are writing it, by choosing a style (a "persona");
- to **translate or summarise text shown on the screen**, and to obtain a suggested reply to a message you have received;
- to use **Assisted Reading**, which overlays a translation on the text shown on the screen;
- to **dictate** a text instead of typing it.

Depending on your device and on your choice, processing takes place **on your phone** or **on remote servers**. Exactly how your data circulates is described in our privacy policy: `https://readit0.github.io/plume-legal/politique-confidentialite`.

**Plume produces text by means of an artificial intelligence system.** The result is a suggestion: it may be inaccurate, clumsy or unsuited to the context. You remain the author of, and responsible for, the text you decide to send. Always re-read before sending.

## Article 6 — Quotas

Use of the service is capped, in order to keep the cost of processing under control.

**Free tier.** You have a base allowance of rewrites per day, without having to watch anything. Once that base allowance is used up, you **may choose** to watch a rewarded ad to unlock additional uses, up to a maximum number of ads per day and an absolute daily cap. Watching an ad is **never compulsory**: if you do not do so, you simply keep what you are entitled to, and you get your base allowance back the next day.

*At the time of writing, the values applied are: a base allowance of 5 rewrites per day, +5 per validated rewarded ad, a maximum of 5 ads per day, and an absolute cap of 30 per day.*

**Subscriber tier.** The subscription lifts the free tier's cap and removes the advertising.

- Where processing takes place **on your device** (devices compatible with the On-device AI): **unlimited use**, subject only to the technical limits of your phone (battery, heat).
- Where processing takes place **on our servers**: use is capped per day and per month, because each call has a real cost. *At the time of writing: 1,000 per day and 9,000 per month.*

**Points common to all tiers:**

- A **screen analysis** (translation or suggested reply) is more expensive than a simple rewrite and consumes **several quota units** — currently 5.
- Quotas are **applied by our servers**, before any processing, and reset when the day (UTC time) or the month changes.
- Quotas are attached to **your account**, not to your device: changing phone or reinstalling the application does not reset them.

**These values may change.** We may adjust them to preserve the economic balance of the service or in the event of a change in the cost of the AI providers. **The figures in force are those displayed in the application**, which are authoritative: the quota gauge and the subscription screen show your actual balance at all times. Any significant reduction in the quotas of an ongoing subscription will be notified to you in advance and will entitle you to cancel free of charge.
