## Two ways to delete your account

### 1. From the application (the fastest)

**Settings → Privacy → Delete my data**

The deletion is **carried out immediately**. It is not placed in a queue and does not wait for any deferred processing: when the application confirms the deletion to you, it has already taken place and has been verified by the server.

### 2. Without installing the application

If you have already uninstalled Plume, or if you no longer have access to your phone, write to:

**sogacmoi7@gmail.com**

- Subject: **Plume account deletion**
- State the **e-mail address of your Plume account** (the one used to sign up, or your Google address if you signed in with Google).

We check that the request does indeed come from the account holder, then we proceed with the deletion. **We handle these requests within a maximum of 30 days**, and generally within a few days. You receive a confirmation by e-mail once the deletion has been carried out.

## What is deleted

The deletion permanently erases:

- **your account** (e-mail address, password, session);
- **your usage counters** — the number of rewrites used per day and per month;
- **your suggestions** — the proposals you may have sent us from the application. This is the only free text we store;
- **your request identifiers** — the technical references of the rewrites requested;
- **your credits and quota unlocks** — purchased credits not yet used, unlocks obtained through advertising, adjusted caps;
- **the link between your devices and your account** — your other phones or tablets are unlinked and become ordinary anonymous devices again.

The device from which you request the deletion is **neutralised**: its link to your account is erased, its identification key is destroyed and replaced by a dead value, and its language and application version are erased. All that remains is an opaque number, which no longer makes it possible to identify you or to find the device.

**We have never stored your texts.** Neither the texts you have rewritten, nor the text read from the screen by Assisted Reading: they were not kept anywhere on our servers, so there is nothing to delete.

**On your phone**, your personas, your avatars, your settings and your per-application rules are stored locally. They are erased by the deletion carried out from the application, and in any event **they disappear when you uninstall Plume**.

---

## What is kept, and why

Three categories of records remain, but **the link with your identity is severed**: your account identifier and your device identifier are removed from them. These records no longer make it possible to trace you.

| What remains | Why we cannot destroy it |
|---|---|
| **Your purchase history** (Google Play transaction identifier, amount, dates, subscription status) | Accounting obligation: proof of payment cannot be destroyed. It is kept **detached from your identity**. |
| **Entitlements acquired by purchase** (subscription, packs) | Same accounting reason, and proof of payment in the event of a dispute. Kept **without any user identifier**. |
| **Advertising rewards and credits already granted** | Prevents the same ad or the same purchase from being cashed in twice. Only the unique reference of the transaction is kept, **without any device identifier**. |
| **Technical security signals** (repeated overruns, integrity check failures) | Fraud prevention. These records contain **no text** and are kept **without any device identifier**. |

This anonymised data is kept for as long as our legal obligations require, in particular our accounting obligations, and is then deleted or aggregated.

## What deletion does not do

**It does not cancel your subscription.** A subscription is managed by Google Play, not by us: deleting your Plume account does not stop it and **you would continue to be charged**.

**Cancel your subscription first**, then delete your account:
Play Store → Menu → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions → Plume → Cancel subscription.

**It does not erase the data held by Google** (purchases, advertising, the phone's speech recognition). That data belongs to your Google account and is managed from your Google account settings.

---

## After deletion

The deletion is **final and irreversible**. We cannot restore a deleted account, nor your personas, nor the purchases attached to that account.

You can create an account again later with the same e-mail address: it will be a **completely new** account, with no history. Your previous purchases, having been detached from any identity, **cannot be restored** to that new account. If you want to keep an ongoing subscription, do not delete your account.
