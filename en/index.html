Your styles, not a menu's

Here, a writing style is called a persona. Some come free, more are waiting in the catalogue.

Free

Direct

Straight to the point. Short sentences, confident tone, no fluff. Ideal for quick decisions, no-nonsense work replies, or clarifying a request.

Professional

Composed and polished, clean vocabulary, the right courtesies. Ideal for a work email, a client message, or any image-sensitive situation.

Friendly

Warm and close, like a friend who has your back. Keeps it natural, adds kindness. Ideal for loved ones, everyday messages, defusing with a smile.

Concise

Trimmed to the essentials without losing meaning. Cuts the filler, keeps the point. Ideal for quick replies, notifications, when every word counts.

Romantic

Tender and sincere, feeling said in simple words. Warms things up without overdoing it, keeps the restraint. Ideal for a love message, a sweet note, a declaration that rings true.

Professional expertise With the subscription

Diplomat

Rephrases with tact and diplomacy. Softens tense messages, keeps the substance but smooths the form. Ideal for delicate exchanges, unhappy clients, disagreements.

Customer service

Professional, empathetic and solution-focused. Acknowledges the issue, reassures, offers a clear next step. Ideal for support, complaints, after-sales.

Tradesperson · quote

Clear, concrete and reassuring about the job. Details the work, frames price and timing with confidence. Ideal for replying to a client, sending a quote, explaining a service.

Calm negotiator

Firm on substance, flexible on form. Defends your position without tension, seeks common ground. Ideal for discussing a price, setting a limit, handling a dispute.

Sale listing

Makes people want it in a few lines. Highlights the product, factual and appealing, with a clear call to action. Ideal for selling online, a classified ad, a second-hand item.

Creativity & style With the subscription

Refined literary

Raises the style: rich vocabulary, flowing sentences, controlled elegance. Ideal for a polished text, a memorable letter, a refined statement.

Wit & repartee

Sharp and witty, a well-placed touch of humour. Comes back with finesse, never hurtful. Ideal for livening up a chat, replying with flair, shining in writing.

Chivalrous

Noble and courteous, a tone of honour and respect. Phrases things with grandeur and gallantry. Ideal for a solemn message, a heartfelt thank-you, an assumed theatrical tone.

Poetic

Imagery and musicality: soft metaphors, crafted rhythm. Turns the plain into the pretty. Ideal for a tender note, a dedication, a message that lands.

Dark & elegant

Dark and refined, mastered mystery and intensity. Dramatic yet controlled. Ideal for a strong mood, a text with character, a striking signature.

Minimalist

Absolute purity: few words, lots of meaning. Every term is chosen. Ideal for a short bio, a tagline, a clean modern message.

Interpretation

More than rephrasing: it captures your intent and renders it clearly. Ideal when your text is rough and you want to say things just right.

Your messages say something else

What you write is replaced by an innocuous sentence. Your contact, if they have Plume, recovers the real message.

Single tap on the capsule

Disguises what you just wrote. “I love you with all my heart” becomes “I had a third helping of gratin”.

Long press on the capsule

Decodes what's shown on screen. It's the gesture that makes a camouflaged message you received readable again.

A secret code, just for the two of you

Every word you write is replaced by a real word from a theme you choose. Decoding retrieves your exact sentence, word for word — never an approximation.

It's a writing game, not a vault. Your text passes through our servers to be camouflaged, and any Plume user can decode it. Don't trust it with anything you wouldn't put on a postcard.

You choose where your text is processed

Permissions, and what Plume does not do

Plume uses the Android accessibility service to read and replace the text in the field you are writing in, when you tap the capsule, and only in the apps you linked yourself. It sends nothing in the background, and it never sends a message for you.

Free, subscription optional

Plume is free to use, with a number of rewrites per day; a rewarded ad unlocks more whenever you decide, never forced on you. The subscription raises the caps, opens the whole catalogue and removes the ads.
