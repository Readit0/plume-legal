The gesture

Plume puts a floating capsule on top of the apps you choose. Everywhere else, it stays invisible.

Tap

Your text is rewritten in the current style, or translated.

Long press

Plume suggests a reply to what is on your screen.

Mic

You speak, your text lands in the input field.

Your styles, not a menu's

Here, a writing style is called a persona: Direct, Professional, Friendly, Concise, and more in the catalogue — diplomacy, sales ad, poetic.

With the subscription, you create your own: Plume then writes like you, not like everyone else.

One style per app

Assign a persona or a mode to each app: your messaging app in Friendly, your work network in Professional. The right tone applies the moment you open it.

Assisted Reading

Translate what you read: manga, webtoons, foreign apps. The translation appears on top of the text, in its place: you read in your language without copying anything.

Plume speaks 60 languages and translates into as many.

You choose where your text is processed

Local mode On your phone

Local mode translates on your phone: nothing is sent, it works offline — but not every language is equally covered there.

Cloud AI Your persona's tone

Cloud AI mode applies your persona's tone and covers those languages; your text then travels to an AI service located outside the European Union, for the time of the processing. Plume keeps none of it.

On-device AI Subscription

The subscription adds an AI that runs on the device: offline, no quota.

Permissions, and what Plume does not do

Plume uses the Android accessibility service to read and replace the text in the field you are writing in, when you tap the capsule, and only in the apps you linked yourself. Assisted Reading asks for your screen capture consent again at every session. It sends nothing in the background, and it never sends a message for you.
